# SHFT Robotics: Google Search Ads

## Is Google Search worth it for recruiting? Short answer: mostly no, with one exception.

**Why Search is weak for this:**
- Almost nobody searches "robotics club `[FILL: city]`." The intent volume is tiny in a single town. You can have a perfect ad and get 5 impressions a week.
- Your buyers (parents and teens) are not actively searching for you. They do not know SHFT exists yet. This is a **demand-creation** problem, and demand creation lives on Meta, not Search.
- On a $5/day budget, Search spreads too thin to ever exit learning or gather signal.

**Where your money should go:** ~90% Meta (Instagram + Facebook), ~10% optional Google. See `meta-ads.md`.

**The one exception worth a tiny budget:** a **branded + local-intent safety net**. Run a small Search campaign so that:
1. When someone hears "SHFT robotics" and Googles it, you own the top result (cheap, high intent).
2. The rare high-intent local searcher ("FRC team near me") finds you instead of a generic club.

**If you run it:** cap at **$2 to $3/day**, exact and phrase match only, single ad group, and check it weekly. If it spends under $5 total in two weeks, it is doing its job quietly. If it spends nothing, pause it and move the budget to Meta. Skip it entirely until the site has a clear "Join SHFT" page that loads fast.

## Local keyword list (start small, exact + phrase)

Group these in ONE ad group. Swap `[FILL: city]` for the real city and the nearest larger city.

**Local intent:**
- robotics club `[FILL: city]`
- FRC team near me
- high school engineering club
- robotics team `[FILL: city]`
- FIRST robotics `[FILL: city]`
- robotics for teens `[FILL: city]`
- after school STEM `[FILL: city]`
- high school robotics Ontario

**Branded (cheap, must-own):**
- SHFT robotics
- SHFT FRC
- shftrobotics

**Negative keywords (add immediately):**
- jobs, hiring, salary, kit, buy, parts, course, university, college, toy, Lego (unless you want LEGO-curious clicks), remote

Match types: put branded terms on `[exact]` and `"phrase"`. Local terms on `"phrase"`. No broad match on this budget.

## Settings if you do run it
- **Campaign type:** Search only. Turn OFF "Search Network partners" and "Display Network."
- **Bidding:** Maximize clicks with a manual max CPC cap of ~$1.50 to start. Switch to Maximize conversions only after you have a tracked RSVP form.
- **Location:** target by radius (see targeting-and-tracking.md), set to "People in or regularly in" your area (not "interested in").
- **Schedule:** review weekly, not daily. Reallocate to Meta if volume stays near zero.
