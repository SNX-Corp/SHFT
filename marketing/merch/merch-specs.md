## Brand color (read this before ordering anything)

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB |
|------|------|-----|-----|
| Signature accent | SHFT Lime | `#A6FF00` | 166, 255, 0 |
| Base dark | SHFT Black | `#1A1A1A` | 26, 26, 26 |
| Base light | White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 |

**The lime is a fluorescent yellow-green ("volt").** Plain CMYK process cannot reproduce it. It will print flat and olive. To get the real color you must use one of:

- **Spot / Pantone ink.** Proof against **Pantone 802 C** or **Pantone 809 C** (both bright fluoro green-yellows). These are the starting reference, not a guaranteed match.
- **Neon / fluorescent screen-print ink** for garments. This is the truest match on fabric.
- **Fluorescent embroidery thread** for caps and patches (e.g. a neon-yellow-green poly thread; match to swatch).

**Always confirm against a physical swatch before a full run.** Neon greens shift hard between screen, paper proof, and fabric. Approve a real-fabric strike-off, not a PDF.

**Contrast rules:** Best look is always lime on black. Lime on white is legible but loses punch (weight it heavier or outline it). Never put the lime bolt on a lime field. Avoid lime on mid-grey.

## 1. Tee: the hero piece

**Garment recommendation:** Black 100% combed ring-spun cotton, mid-weight (approx 4.3–5.3 oz), retail fit. Suggested blanks: Bella+Canvas 3001, Comfort Colors 1717 (garment-dyed black), or Gildan Softstyle 64000 for budget. Confirm the black is a true deep black, not washed charcoal.

**Print location:** Full front, centered on the chest.

**Placement:** Center the wordmark horizontally. Top of the wordmark sits about 3 in (7.5 cm) below the collar seam on an adult M. Scale proportionally up/down per size if the shop runs size-specific screens; otherwise lock one placement to size M.

**Print size:** Wordmark approx **10–11 in (25–28 cm) wide** across the chest on adult M. Keep clear space around it equal to the bolt height on all sides.

**Colors / inks:**
- Letters: White (standard white plastisol).
- Bolt (the "I"): SHFT Lime `#A6FF00`, **neon/fluorescent ink**, proof to Pantone 802 C / 809 C.
- 2-color print: white + neon lime on black. No halftones needed.

**Copy (optional, under the wordmark):** Small line in the Orbitron-style face, uppercase, tight tracking, white or lime:
> ONTARIO COMMUNITY FRC TEAM

Or, once known, the team number:
> TEAM [FILL: team number]

**Optional back hit:** Small lime bolt at center-back yoke (2 in tall) or the tagline `SHIFT THE STANDARD.` across the upper back.

## 2. Bolt sticker / patch: the most reusable item

The lime bolt alone. This is the most recognizable, most repeatable piece of the brand. Make a lot of these.

### Sticker
**Type:** Die-cut vinyl sticker, glossy or matte, contour-cut to the bolt silhouette (or a rounded-rect with the bolt on black).

**Size:** 2.5–3 in (6–7.5 cm) tall for laptops/water bottles. Offer a 1.5 in mini for helmets/phones.

**Colors:**
- Die-cut bolt: solid SHFT Lime `#A6FF00` on transparent (or on a black square).
- Print method: digital + spot fluorescent, or solid neon vinyl cut. Confirm the vinyl green matches the swatch.

### Patch (woven or embroidered)
**Type:** Embroidered or woven patch, iron-on or velcro-back for pit shirts and bags.

**Size:** 2.5–3 in (6–7.5 cm) tall.

**Colors:** 2 colors only: lime bolt + black background/border. Use fluorescent poly thread for the bolt. Merrow or laser-cut border in black.

**Copy:** None. The bolt carries it. (Optional tiny `SHFT` woven below if a wider patch is wanted.)

**Garment recommendation:** Black heavyweight pullover hoodie, 50/50 or ring-spun cotton-rich fleece (approx 8.5 oz). Suggested blanks: Independent Trading IND4000, Gildan 18500, or Comfort Colors 1567 for a premium garment-dyed black.

**Print locations:**
- **Left chest (small):** Lime bolt only, approx **3.5–4 in (9–10 cm) tall**, positioned left-chest (about 7 in down from the shoulder seam, 4 in in from center).

**Colors / inks:**
- Left chest bolt: SHFT Lime `#A6FF00`, neon ink.
- Back wordmark: White letters + lime bolt (2-color), OR all-lime tagline.
- Proof lime to Pantone 802 C / 809 C, confirm on a physical fleece swatch (texture changes the read).

**Copy options for the back:**

> TOP 10 IN ONTARIO. YEAR ONE.

## 4. Cap

**Garment recommendation:** Black structured 6-panel or unstructured dad cap. Suggested blanks: Flexfit 6277, Richardson 112 (trucker, black/black), or a black 5-panel. Keep it all-black so the lime pops.

**Decoration:** Embroidery (not print) for a cap.

**Front (primary):** Embroidered lime bolt, centered front panel, approx **2–2.5 in (5–6 cm) tall**. Fluorescent yellow-green poly thread, matched to swatch. 3D puff optional on the bolt for extra punch (test first, since puff can distort thin angles).

**Alternative front:** Small embroidered `SH⚡FT` wordmark, approx 3.5 in (9 cm) wide, white thread letters + lime bolt.

**Side / back (optional):** Tiny lime bolt on the left side panel or wearer's-left back, 1 in tall.

**Colors:** Lime bolt thread + black cap. Embroidery is inherently 2-color here. Keep it that way.

## 5. Pit banner

**Use:** Hangs at the back of the pit / above the team table at competition. Readable from across the venue.

**Material:** 13 oz matte vinyl banner with hemmed edges and grommets every 2 ft, OR a fabric tension banner for a cleaner look. Matte to kill arena-light glare.

**Size:** Standard **6 ft × 3 ft (183 × 91 cm)** landscape. Go 8 ft × 4 ft if the pit space and budget allow.

**Layout:**
- Background: solid SHFT Black `#1A1A1A` (or true black).
- Center: large `SH⚡FT` wordmark, white letters + lime bolt, sized to roughly 60% of banner width with bolt-height clear space around it.
- Under the wordmark: tagline `SHIFT THE STANDARD.` in the Orbitron-style face, uppercase, tight tracking, lime or white.
- Bottom strip: `[FILL: team number]` · `ONTARIO` · `shftrobotics.com` · `@shftrobotics`. Small, white, evenly spaced.
- Optional: faint lime energy-pattern motif (see `energy-pattern.svg`) bled off the corners at low opacity. Keep it subtle so the wordmark stays the hero.

**Colors / inks:**
- Large-format print. Specify lime `#A6FF00` and **flag that it is fluorescent**. Ask the shop whether their large-format machine can hit a neon green or whether they should run a spot/fluoro pass. Standard eco-solvent/UV will read duller than the screen.
- Proof to Pantone 802 C / 809 C, request a small color-strip proof before printing the full banner.

## Clear space, minimum size, and don'ts

- **Clear space:** keep open space around the wordmark equal to the height of the bolt on all sides. Don't crowd it with seams, other logos, or text.
- **Minimum sizes:** wordmark no smaller than 1.5 in (3.8 cm) wide in print; bolt no smaller than 0.5 in (1.3 cm) tall.
- **Embroidery:** simplify to 2 colors (lime + garment color). The bolt embroiders cleanly on its own; the full wordmark needs a tested digitized file.
- ❌ Don't recolor the bolt anything but the lime (or a single garment-matched knockout for tonal looks).
- ❌ Don't rebuild the wordmark in a substitute font. Use the supplied/true-vector art only.
- ❌ Don't stretch, skew, or add drop shadows/outlines to the wordmark.
- ❌ Don't run process CMYK lime and call it done. It will be olive.
