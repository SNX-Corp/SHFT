# SHFT Captions: Fall 2026 push (brand, program, events, WCP, lab)

24 captions for Instagram and Facebook across the six pillars Malhar named: the
brand, the program, the off-season event, WCP, the open house, and the September
lab setup. Copy a block, fill every `[FILL: ...]`, post.

**House rules (these gate every post)**
- Passed through the shft-public gate: mentor voice, no superlatives, goals
  stated as goals, no invented facts. Keep it that way when you edit.
- No em dashes. One CTA per post. First line must stand alone.
- Anything marked `[FILL: ...]` is a real fact only you can supply. Anything
  marked `VERIFY:` needs your confirmation before the post ships.
- **Real footage rule:** WCP warehouse, lab setup, and event recap posts go up
  only with real photos or video from the actual day. Never stock, never AI
  stand-ins presented as our space.
- **Never name ALT-F4, Team 7558, or Noeen in any SHFT post, caption, comment,
  reply, tag, or image.** Mentor experience is always unattributed: "our mentors
  have coached FRC and VEX for more than ten years." WCP may be named.
- VERIFY before first use: workshop address (26 Mallard Rd, North York) and the
  grades range (8 to 12) both come from draft recruitment materials. Confirm
  both before they appear in a public post.

**Hashtag sets**
- **CORE:** `#SHFT #FRC #FIRSTRobotics #OntarioRobotics #STEM`
- **LOCAL:** `#NorthYork #Toronto #TorontoSTEM #OntarioFIRST`
- Instagram: CORE + LOCAL. Facebook: no hashtags, or two at most.

**Instagram vs Facebook**
- Instagram reaches students and shows legitimacy to everyone who checks the
  grid. Short captions, strong first line, Reels and carousels.
- Facebook reaches parents. Longer, calmer posts that answer real questions.
  Post to the SHFT page first, then share into local parent and community
  groups where group rules allow it. The Facebook variants below exist for this.

## Pillar 2: The program

### prog-01 (what students learn)
**First line:** What a student actually learns here.

CAD design, metal fabrication, electrical wiring, programming, and how to run a
project with real deadlines. Fall is training, 3 to 4 days a week in the
workshop. By January the team is designing and building a full competition
robot together.

No experience needed walking in. We teach from zero.

**CTA:** The full program is at shftrobotics.com.

`#SHFT #FRC #FIRSTRobotics #OntarioRobotics #STEM`

---

### prog-02 (the CAD block)
**First line:** Four months on CAD before the robot exists.

From September to December, students learn computer-aided design, the same
category of software professional engineers use to design real parts. The goal
is every student fluent before build season opens in January, so nobody watches
from the side when it counts.

**CTA:** Program details at shftrobotics.com.

`#SHFT #FRC #FIRSTRobotics #OntarioRobotics #CAD #STEM`

---

### prog-03 (what the fee covers)
**First line:** One fee covers the season. Here is what's in it.

The robot, registration for two district events and the provincial
championship, tools, parts, the workshop, food during build sessions, robot
transport, and coaching. The only costs on top are hotels and travel if the
team reaches the provincial championship or the world championship.

We go through the numbers with every family in person, line by line, before
anyone commits.

**CTA:** Ask us anything at team@shftrobotics.com.

`#SHFT #FRC #FIRSTRobotics #OntarioRobotics #STEM`

### prog-04 (the roster of 20)
**First line:** We're building a first roster of 20 students.

Twenty is deliberate. It's small enough that every student works on the actual
robot, and every family knows the mentors by name. Grades 8 to 12, any
experience level, including none.

We meet with every family before anyone commits, so you know exactly what the
season asks before you say yes.

**CTA:** Start the conversation at shftrobotics.com.

`#SHFT #FRC #FIRSTRobotics #OntarioRobotics #NorthYork`

in Ontario, and we'll post our results either way.
