# SHFT Sponsorship Package: Research and Plan

## What strong FRC teams do well

The best sponsor materials do four jobs fast:

1. Prove the team is credible.
2. Make the sponsor's recognition easy to understand.
3. Show where the money goes.
4. Give a clear next step.

Research notes:

- MadTown Robotics, Team 1323, makes scale visible. Their about page says the program grew from 4 students and one mentor to 50 students and around 50 active sponsors. Their public sponsor structure includes Platinum at $5,000+, Gold at $2,500 to $4,999, Silver at $1,000 to $2,499, Bronze at $100 to $999, plus in-kind sponsors.
- The Blair Robot Project, Team 449, uses simple cumulative tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Title Sponsor. Higher tiers add robot/logo visibility, official FIRST listing, and possible workplace robot visits.
- f(x) Robotics, Team 6004, leans into sponsor experiences: shop tours, meet-and-greets, team workshops, company event participation, pit access, and social shoutouts.
- Titan Robotics, Team 5587, keeps its packet concise. Benefits are cumulative and focus on clear deliverables: appreciation letter, social post, website, pit logo, robot logo, workshop tour, and robot demo.
- Valence Robotics, Team 8429, uses regular sponsor updates as a repeated benefit across tiers. This is a good pattern for SHFT because a new team can create momentum even before trophies.
- FRC 167 documentation recommends 3 to 5 sponsor tiers, round memorable amounts, visible benefits, season updates, and consistent tracking in a master sponsor spreadsheet.

## Positioning for SHFT

SHFT should not sound like a club asking for a favor. It should sound like a young technical program offering a sharp, visible community partnership.

Core line:

> Partner with the Ontario FRC team built to compete from day one.

Sponsor value:

- Brand visibility: robot, pit, shirts, website, social.
- Community impact: students building real engineering skills.
- Talent pipeline: sponsor connects early with future engineers, designers, programmers, and operators.
- Real moments: robot demos, open house, shop visit, sponsor field day.
- Founder status: early sponsors are not just listed. They are part of the origin story.

## Recommended tiers

Use six tiers in the draft because SHFT is new and needs room for families, small businesses, and larger corporate partners.

| Tier | Amount | Role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Founding Partner | $10,000+ | Premier relationship, strongest visibility, sponsor demo |
| Platinum | $5,000+ | Major season support, large robot and pit visibility |
| Gold | $2,500+ | Serious local business tier |
| Silver | $1,000+ | Accessible business sponsor |
| Bronze | $500+ | Community sponsor |
| Community | $250+ | Family, alumni, small local supporters |

In-kind should count toward tier value when it offsets real season costs: aluminum, polycarbonate, fasteners, machining, printing, food, transportation, tools, batteries, electronics, space, or event support.

## Package structure

1. Cover: big SHFT sponsor statement.
2. Why this matters: FRC, student impact, team promise.
3. What sponsors fund: season budget categories.
4. Sponsor tiers: clean matrix.
5. Experiences and deliverables: demos, open house, company events, updates.
6. Next step: contact, QR placeholder, sponsor info checklist.

## Brand direction

Stay strict:

- Dark primary ground: `#1A1A1A`.
- Green only as `#A6FF00`.
- Square edges.
- Orbitron for impact headings.
- Exo 2 for labels and tier names.
- Raleway for sponsor-facing body copy.
- No soft gradients, no rounded cards, no stock sponsor imagery.
- Use dot grids, hard divider lines, and bolt marks.

## Launch path

1. Fill team number, city, legal donation recipient, payment instructions, tax receipt language, and contact.
2. Replace QR placeholder with a sponsor interest form.
3. Export PDF from `sponsors/sponsor-package.html`.
4. Add to the design hub Sponsors tab.
5. Once approved, add a polished public sponsor page to the live Astro site.
