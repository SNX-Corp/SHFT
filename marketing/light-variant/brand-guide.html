03 / Logo

Logo and Marks

The primary mark is the S-Mark from SS.svg: an ink top hook and bottom hook plus a lime diagonal connector. That lime diagonal is the only color in the logo. Pair the dark wordmark on light, the light wordmark on dark.

Dark Wordmark / On White
Light Wordmark / On #1A1A1A
  • Clear space. Keep open space around the mark equal to the height of the S-mark on all sides.
  • Minimum sizes. S-mark 24px. Wordmark 80px wide.
  • Color. Keep the lime diagonal as the only color. Ink hooks on light, white hooks on dark.
  • Vector. The S-Mark source is SS.svg. The maple-leaf crest is the secondary Canadian accent.
04 / Principles

Design Principles

01

Sharp, Not Soft

Angular geometry, no rounded corners, aggressive crops. Softness signals uncertainty.

border-radius: 0
02

White Canvas

Let white space work. Color is accent, not background. The neon green earns attention by being rare.

bg: #FFFFFF
03

Motion With Purpose

Every animation serves hierarchy. Nothing moves just to move.

ease: expo.out
04

Type First

Typography carries the brand. When in doubt, make the text bigger.

font-weight: 900
