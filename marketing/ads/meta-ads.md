Recruitment push for a new Ontario FRC team. Small budget, local reach.

**Brand:** Lime `#A6FF00` on near-black `#0D0D0D`. Wordmark and lime bolt.
**Site:** https://shftrobotics.com · **Instagram:** @shftrobotics
**Primary CTA button (Meta):** `Learn More` (use `Sign Up` only once a real form or info-night RSVP page is live).

---

## How to structure this in Ads Manager

- **1 Campaign:** objective `Engagement` or `Leads` (pick `Leads` once you have an RSVP form; otherwise `Traffic` to the site).
- **2 Ad Sets:** Audience A (parents) and Audience B (teens), so you can budget and read results separately.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements OFF at first. Hand-pick Instagram Feed, Instagram Stories, Facebook Feed. Skip Audience Network (low-quality clicks on a tiny budget).
- **Optimization:** Landing page views (not link clicks) so Meta finds people who actually load the site.

## Creative direction

Keep it unmistakably SHFT: black background, lime bolt, hard contrast, no stock-photo clichés.

**Static (1080x1080):** Use `ad-creative.html` in this folder. Black canvas, oversized lime bolt, a single bold hook ("Build robots. Compete. Win.") and the wordmark. One creative per audience with a swapped hook.

**Story / Reel (1080x1920):** Same system, vertical. Add the info-night line at the bottom: "`[FILL: info night date/time]` · `[FILL: info night location]`".

**Video (best performer, 8 to 15s):** Fast cuts of build energy. Sparks, a drill driving a screw, a robot moving, hands on metal, students laughing. Hard cut to black, lime bolt snaps on, wordmark lands, then the CTA card "Join SHFT". Phone footage is fine and reads more authentic than polish. Subtitles on (most views are muted).

**Rules:**
- Lime is the only accent. Everything else is black, white, or grayscale.
- Lead with motion or the bolt in the first second.
- One message per creative. Do not stack offers.
- Logo lockup safe area: keep the bolt and wordmark clear of the bottom 250px where Meta overlays the CTA on Stories.

## Budget + run plan

Total test budget: **about $105 over 3 weeks** (`$5/day`).

| Week | Daily | Split | Goal |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| 1 | $5/day | $2.50 A / $2.50 B | Learn which audience and creative gets cheap clicks. Do not touch it. Let Meta exit the learning phase. |
| 2 | $5/day | Shift to the winner (e.g. $3.50 / $1.50) | Kill the worst ad in each set. Add one new hook to the winning set. |
| 3 | $5/day, then boost | Concentrate on the winner | If an info night is set, raise to $8 to $10/day for the final 4 days before the event, ending the night before. |

**When to boost:** when one ad is clearly under your target cost per landing-page view (see targeting-and-tracking.md) and the info-night date is inside 10 days. Boost reach, not new creative.

**Organic multiplier (free):** Post the same creatives to @shftrobotics. Boost the single best-performing organic post for $10 to $20 as a one-off; boosted posts that already have likes and comments convert better than cold ads.

**Do not:**
- Run for under 3 days then judge it. The first 48 hours are noise.
- Edit a live ad set daily. Every edit resets learning.
- Spread $5/day across 6 ads. Two audiences, two to three creatives each, maximum.
