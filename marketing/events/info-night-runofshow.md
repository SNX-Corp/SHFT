## Segment notes

### Welcome (5 min)
- Open with the brand line, not a bio. "Shift the standard."
- One sentence on the mentors' track record: they have taken a team from nothing to the top of Canadian FRC.
- Tell people exactly what the next 50 minutes look like so nobody is waiting to leave.

### Robot demo (10 min)
- Drive it. A robot sitting still convinces no one.
- Narrate while it moves: what it's doing, how fast it built, who built it.
- Have a student driver, not a mentor, at the controls.

### What you actually do (10 min)
- Make it concrete and non-intimidating. Show the CAD screen, the wiring, the scouting sheet, the Instagram.
- The message at every station: "You do not need experience to start here. You will."

### Member stories (8 min)
- Pick students who are genuinely excited, not the most senior.
- Coach them on one beat each: where they started, what they can do now, where it's taking them.

### How to join (7 min)
- The path on one slide: Sign up tonight → First meeting → Onboarding.
- Knock out the three fears before they're asked: it does not cost a fortune, it does not eat your whole life, you do not need to already be good.

### Q&A (10 min)
- Assign a mentor to "field anything financial," one to "field anything about time and safety," one for "college and future."
- If you don't know an answer, say "great question, grab me at the table and I'll get you the exact number."

### Close (5 min)
- The last thing they hear is the CTA. Big QR code on screen. Paper sheet at the door.
- "First meeting is [FILL]. We'll see you there."

## What to bring

**Tech / demo**
- Robot, fully charged, plus backup batteries and charger
- Robot controller / driver station + laptop
- Game pieces for the demo
- Extension cords, power bar, tape (gaff/painters) for cable management
- Projector or large screen, HDMI/adapters, clicker/remote

**Signup + paper**
- Laptop or tablet open to the Google Form
- QR code printed large (table tent + on the slide)
- Printed paper sign-in sheets + pens (plenty)
- Flyers / one-pagers to hand out

**Brand / room**
- SHFT wordmark / banner if available (wordmark.png for dark backgrounds)
- Pull-up banner or printed signage with @shftrobotics and the site URL
- Stickers or small giveaways if you have them

**Display pieces**
- A CAD screenshot or laptop running CAD live
- A wiring board / electrical sample
- A scouting sheet example
- Phone or screen showing the Instagram feed

**People**
- Lead mentor + station mentors (one per sub-team)
- 2–3 student speakers (briefed)
- Drive team for the demo
- 1–2 greeters + 1–2 signup-table staff
