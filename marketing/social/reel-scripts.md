# SHFT Reel Scripts

5 short-form scripts (15 to 30s) for Instagram Reels and TikTok. Built to recruit students. Each one is shot-by-shot with on-screen text, an audio idea, and a ready caption.

**Shooting rules**
- Shoot vertical, 1080x1920.
- First frame must carry a readable hook in under one second.
- On-screen text in Orbitron (heavy, uppercase) for headlines. Lime `#A6FF00` accent on black. Keep text in the safe zone (away from the bottom 250px where the caption sits).
- Keep cuts fast. Aim for a cut every 1 to 2 seconds.
- Always end on the wordmark and the ask.
- No em dashes in any on-screen text or caption.

> Swap `[FILL: ...]` before posting: team number, city, info-night date/time/location.
