JOIN SHFT ROBOTICS.

A competitive FRC robotics team in North York for grades 8 to 12. No experience required. Students design, build, and program a full-size competition robot and compete across Ontario.

WHY SHFT
  1. 01Team Director and Head Coach Malhar Soni; Voluntary Mentor Noeen Kashif. They mentor FRC Team 7558 ALT-F4: 35th in Canada in 2019, 3rd in 2026.
  2. 02Sister-team partnership with Team 7558 ALT-F4: shared designs, joint practice, coordinated scouting.
  3. 03Sponsored by WCP (West Coast Products), whose parts run on competition robots worldwide.
  4. 04A dedicated workshop at 26 Mallard Rd with a full regulation FRC practice field.
  5. 05A four-month CAD course before build season, then real fabrication, wiring, and programming.
  6. 06Every student builds a portfolio: CAD models, code they wrote, and a robot they helped put on the field.
  7. 07Our goal in year one: a top-10 finish in Ontario. We say it out loud because our mentors have made this climb before.
BUILT FROM THE GROUND UP
Every student is trained into a role and coached until they own it, through a season of structured workshops. We don't take it easy.
PART OF THE COMMUNITY
Team SHFT is excited to learn from the FRC community and share resources along the way.
Program Fee
THE PROGRAM FEE IS $8,000 FOR THE YEAR.
It covers registration, parts, tools, the facility, food, and coaching. We meet with every family one-on-one before anyone commits.
