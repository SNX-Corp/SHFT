Parent FAQ

How much does it cost?

We keep it accessible and never turn a student away over money. Exact number at the info night.

How much time does it take?

A few sessions a week, more in build season. We are upfront so families can plan.

Is it safe?

Yes. Trained mentors, safety rules, supervised use of every tool.

Zero experience, is that okay?

Yes. Most members start with none. We teach everything.

Does it help with college?

Real engineering, leadership, and a portfolio admissions and scholarships notice.

Who runs it?

Mentors who have taken a team from nothing to the top of Canadian FRC.

Parent commitment?

Optional. Rides help, and any skill you want to share is welcome.

What if it is not for them?

They try it, no strings. Most stay. If not, they leave with new skills.
