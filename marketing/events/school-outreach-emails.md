## (d) Short DM script: replying to an interested student

Use on Instagram DM (@shftrobotics) or text. Keep it fast and warm.

**First reply:**
> Hey! So glad you're interested in SHFT. We're an Ontario FRC team and we build a real competition robot. No experience needed at all. Want me to send you the details?

**If yes:**
> Awesome. Info night is [FILL: date] at [FILL: time], [FILL: location]. There's a live robot demo and you'll see exactly what you'd be doing. Sign up here so we can save you a spot: [FILL: form link]

**Answering "do I need experience / will I be behind":**
> Nope. Most of our members started with zero experience. We teach you. The only thing you need is that you want to learn.

**Answering "what would I actually do":**
> You pick where you start: build/CAD, programming, electrical, strategy/scouting, or media. Then you learn the rest as you go.

**Soft close:**
> Want me to add you to the list for [FILL: date]? Just drop your name and I'll save you a spot.
