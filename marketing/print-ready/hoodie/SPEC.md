# SHFT ROBOTICS — Pullover Hoodie Print Spec

One page. Hand this to the print vendor. Read the proof.html alongside it for placement.

## Item

Black pullover hoodie, screen printed, 2 spot colors (white + lime).

**Recommended blanks (heavyweight cotton/poly fleece, true black):**

| Blank | Example SKU | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Gildan 18500 Heavy Blend | 18500 BLACK | Budget standard, 50/50, holds screen ink well |
| Independent Trading IND4000 | IND4000 BLACK | Heavier 10 oz, retail feel, preferred |
| Gildan SF500 Softstyle Fleece | SF500 BLACK | Softer hand if a midweight is wanted |

Confirm the exact black on the chosen blank. We want a true black body, not washed/charcoal.

---

## Print Method

- Screen print (plastisol or water-base discharge).
- White must be opaque. On black fleece, run a white underbase if using the lime over any dark area; lime sits on white letters here so the bolt prints directly on garment with a thin white base for pop.
- Soft hand preferred on the back print. Standard plastisol acceptable for the chest.

## Colors

Keep to 2 colors. Do not add a third.

| Role | Color | Print as |
|------|-------|----------|
| Letters / lockup | White `#FFFFFF` | Opaque white ink (with underbase on the back) |
| Bolt + ROBOTICS accent | Lime `#A6FF00` | **Spot color, not CMYK** |

**Lime is critical.** Plain CMYK prints flat olive and is wrong. Run lime as a spot color: proof it against **Pantone 802 C** or **Pantone 809 C**, or use a fluorescent/neon green ink. Always pull a physical ink swatch on the actual black fabric and get it approved before the full run. Screen, monitor, and digital proofs will not represent neon correctly.

## Production Checklist

- [ ] Black blank confirmed, true black (Gildan 18500 / IND4000 / SF500), correct sizes and quantities.
- [ ] Art received as vector. Color separations confirmed: white and lime only.
- [ ] Lime spot ink chosen. Physical swatch on black fabric approved (Pantone 802 C / 809 C or neon). Sign off before run.
- [ ] White underbase planned where needed for opacity and lime pop.
- [ ] Left chest screen: 95 mm wide, 180 mm down, 60 mm left of center.
- [ ] Upper back screen: 300 mm wide, centered, top 80 mm below collar seam.
- [ ] Registration of white and lime checked on the press.
- [ ] First article (one printed hoodie) photographed and approved before full run.
- [ ] Cure temp verified for the chosen ink so prints survive wash.
- [ ] Wash test on the first article.
