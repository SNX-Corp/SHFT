02 / Logo and Marks

Logo and Marks

The primary lockup is the SHFT ROBOTICS wordmark: white "SHFT" with the green lightning bolt as the "I", and "ROBOTICS" letter-spaced below. The bolt on its own is the only mark that runs without the letters. Both sit white and green on the dark ground.

Primary Wordmark / On #1A1A1A
Bolt / Standalone Mark
Retired: the S-mark

The angular arrow mark that read as an "S", its four brand-board variations, and the version set inside a green circle are no longer part of the identity. Do not use them and do not accept them back on a vendor proof. The bolt covers every placement they used to.

  • Clear space. Keep open space on all four sides equal to a quarter of the mark's height.
  • Minimum sizes. Bolt 24px tall. Wordmark 80px wide.
  • Color. Keep the bolt and "ROBOTICS" in green #A6FF00. White letters on dark, never recolor the bolt.
  • Primary lockup. Use the SHFT ROBOTICS wordmark as the default. The bolt stands alone for avatars, stickers, and watermarks.
  • Production files. Printers, embroiderers, and apparel vendors get the pack at shftrobotics.com/brand#downloads, not the images on this page.

04 / Two Looks

Dark Primary, Light Variant

The brand runs on a near-black and white system with one green accent. Both looks share the rule: square edges, border-radius: 0.

DarkPrimary

Ground #1A1A1A, white type, green accent. Matches the real hoodies, banner, flag, robot, and social. The marketing kit ships in this look.

LightVariant

Ground #FFFFFF, ink #1A1A1A type, green used sparingly. This is the website style, preserved in the light variant kit.

05 / Patterns

Brand Patterns

Use lightly, never noisy. Keep green at low opacity on the dark ground so the type stays the hero.

Dot Grid

A regular grid of small green dots at 8 to 22 percent opacity. Backgrounds and texture fields.

Speed Slashes

Three parallel diagonal bars. A corner accent for energy and motion. Hard angles, never curved.
