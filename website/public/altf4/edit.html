How this works. Every box below is one piece of text on the sponsor page. Type, and the preview on the right updates instantly. Wrap a word in *stars* for red, or **double** for white. Press Enter for a line break. Changed boxes show a red edge. When it looks right, hit Publish and it goes live, or Download JSON to save the file yourself. Nothing is public until you publish.
