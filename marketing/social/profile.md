## Link-in-bio structure

Use one link tool (Linktree, Beacons, or a simple `/links` page on shftrobotics.com). Order matters: most-wanted action at the top. Suggested stack:

1. **Join SHFT** (the application or interest form). Primary, top, bold label.
2. **Info night RSVP** for `[FILL: date]` (swap to "Recap" after it passes).
3. **What is FRC? (60-second explainer)** for parents and the unsure.
4. **Meet the team.** Link to a highlight reel or the team page.
5. **shftrobotics.com**, the main site.
6. **Email us / DM us** for low-friction contact and questions.

**Rules**
- Never more than 6 links. Decision fatigue kills clicks.
- The top link should always be the single thing you most want a stranger to do this week.
- Update link 1 and 2 every phase: recruiting → info night → tryouts → "joined? here's what's next."

## Story-highlight covers

Covers should be **lime bolt or Orbitron initial on black**, consistent across all highlights so the profile reads as one clean row. Keep icons simple and high-contrast. Suggested highlights, in order:

| Order | Highlight | Cover icon idea | Holds |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 | **JOIN** | Lime bolt | The pitch, how to join, the link. Pin the best recruiting frames. |
| 2 | **INFO NIGHT** | Calendar / door | Date, time, location, RSVP, recap after. |
| 3 | **TEAM** | People / star | Member intros, roles, faces. |
| 4 | **BUILD** | Wrench / gear | Robot progress, shop clips, subsystems. |
| 5 | **TOOLS** | Drill | "No experience needed" teaching clips. |
| 6 | **COMPETE** | Trophy / flag | Competition hype, goals, event info. |
| 7 | **FAQ** | Question mark | Common questions: cost, time, experience, grades. |

**Cover production:** export 7 covers from the same template. Black `#1A1A1A` background, one lime `#A6FF00` icon centered. Keep the icon in the top third of the 1080x1920 frame so it is not cropped by the avatar circle.

## Pinned-post plan (Instagram pins 3 posts)

The three pinned slots are the first thing every new visitor sees. They should answer: **what is this, can I do it, and what do I do next.**

| Pin | Post | Why it's pinned |
|-----|------|-----------------|
| **Pin 1** | The "Top 10 in Ontario. Year one." hype Reel (reel-scripts Reel 3) | Hooks attention and states the mission in 16 seconds. |
| **Pin 2** | The "POV: you just joined a robotics team" Reel (reel-scripts Reel 1) | Answers "can someone like me do this?" with a yes. Highest recruiting value. |
| **Pin 3** | The info-night / how-to-join post (carousel info-02 or info-night Reel 5) | The conversion. Tells an interested visitor exactly what to do next. |

**Rotation:** during the push, keep Pin 3 as the most time-sensitive call (info night → tryouts → last call). Swap it as deadlines move. Keep Pins 1 and 2 stable since they are evergreen.

**TikTok:** TikTok also allows 3 pinned videos. Mirror the same three (hype, POV, info night).
