# SHFT Content Calendar: Fall 2026 (July to September)

The posting plan for the six pillars: brand, program, off-season event, WCP,
open house, lab setup. Captions live in `captions-fall-2026.md`. The original
recruiting calendar (`content-calendar.md`) still applies for the info-night
push; this calendar runs alongside it and shares the same rules.

Because the event, warehouse, and open-house dates aren't locked yet, this
calendar is keyed to **triggers**, not fixed weeks. When a trigger fires, run
its block. Between triggers, run the steady weekly skeleton.

**Formats:** IG Reels for reach, carousels for depth, stories for day-of
urgency. Facebook gets the two FB variants plus shares of major announcements.
Feed graphics at 4:5 (1080x1350), Reels and stories at 9:16 (1080x1920), and
keep text inside the safe zones (top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6%).

## Steady weekly skeleton (between triggers)

- **Monday:** one post from whichever pillar has real news; otherwise a
  program post.
- **Wednesday or Thursday:** one brand or explainer post.
- **Weekend:** stories only, proof of life.
- **Facebook:** one page post a week minimum; share every major announcement.

## Rules that override everything

- Warehouse, event, and lab posts ship only with real footage from the real
  day. No stock, no AI stand-ins presented as our space.
- Fill every `[FILL]` and clear every `VERIFY:` before a post goes live.
- Never name ALT-F4, 7558, or Noeen anywhere on SHFT channels, including
  comments and replies. WCP may be named.
- One CTA per post. First line stands alone.
