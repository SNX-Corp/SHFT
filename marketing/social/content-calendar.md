# SHFT Social Content Calendar

A 4-week recruiting push for **@shftrobotics**. Goal: fill the info night and tryouts with students who think "that could be me." Brand voice: short, declarative, electric. No em dashes.

> **Fill before posting:** team number, city, info-night date/time, info-night location, tryout dates. Anywhere you see `[FILL: ...]`, swap in the real value.

**Content pillars**
1. **Meet the team.** Faces, roles, why people joined.
2. **What we build.** Robots, subsystems, the actual machine.
3. **Behind the scenes.** The shop, late nights, the process.
4. **No experience needed.** Lower the barrier, recruit the unsure.
5. **Competition hype.** The stakes, the goal, "Top 10 in Ontario. Year one."
6. **Info night and recruitment.** The ask, the date, the door.

**Cadence:** 3 to 4 posts per week. Reels carry reach. Carousels carry depth. Stories carry urgency and proof-of-life. Post Reels 6 to 8pm on weekdays. Run a story most days the week of info night.

**Caption reference** points to a labeled block in `captions.md`.

## Repeatable weekly skeleton (after week 4)

If the push extends, rotate this every week:

- **Mon.** Reel, high-reach pillar (hype or no-experience).
- **Wed.** Carousel, depth pillar (what we build or behind the scenes).
- **Fri.** Reel, human pillar (meet the team).
- **Weekend.** Story, proof-of-life (behind the scenes).

**Story habit:** post at least 2 stories a week even on light weeks. Repost any tagged mentions. Keep the team account alive between big posts.

---

## Posting rules of thumb

- **Reels first.** They reach people who do not follow you yet. That is the recruiting engine.
- **One clear ask per post.** Either "follow," "save," or "DM us / link in bio." Never three asks at once.
- **First frame is the whole game.** The on-screen hook decides whether anyone watches. Make it readable in under one second.
- **Caption first line must stand alone.** Feeds truncate after one line. Front-load the hook.
- **Pin the strongest recruiting Reel** (see `profile.md` pinned-post plan).
- **Tag location and partners** (Team 7558 ALT-F4, WCP) when relevant for local reach.
