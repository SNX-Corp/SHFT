# SHFT "Bring a Friend" Member Referral

The best recruiter is a member who loves the team. Keep this simple, trackable, and low-cost. The whole mechanic runs off one field on the signup form.

**Tagline:** Bring someone good. Build the team you want to be on.

---

## How it works (3 steps)

1. **A current member invites a friend** to the info night or to sign up.
2. **The friend signs up** and writes the member's name in the "Referred by" field on the form (field #13 in signup-form.md) or on the paper sheet.
3. **Both get credit** once the friend shows up to their first real meeting (not just signs up). This keeps it honest: a referral counts when the friend actually joins.

---

## The reward ladder

Pick what fits your budget. Recognition beats cash for team culture, so lead with status and small perks.

| Referrals that join | Reward |
|---------------------|--------|
| 1 friend joins | Shoutout on @shftrobotics + your name on the referral leaderboard |
| 2 friends join | SHFT sticker pack / patch [FILL: item] |
| 3 friends join | SHFT shirt or merch item [FILL: item] |
| Top referrer of the recruiting season | A standout perk: [FILL: e.g., first pick of a sub-team role, featured post, team-chosen prize] |

Keep the top prize aspirational but cheap. The leaderboard does most of the work.

---

## Make it easy to share

Give members a ready-to-paste message so there's zero friction:

> Yo, my robotics team SHFT is recruiting. We build a real competition robot, no experience needed. Info night is [FILL: date] at [FILL: location]. Come check it out, and put my name (`[member name]`) in the "referred by" box when you sign up: [FILL: form link]

And a one-line version for stories / group chats:
> Come build robots with me. SHFT info night [FILL: date]. Sign up: [FILL: form link] (put my name down)

---

## Tracking (no extra tools)

- The "Referred by" field already feeds your Google Sheet.
- Add a tab or filter that counts referrals per member, and only count a referral once the friend attends their first meeting.
- Update the leaderboard after each meeting and post it where members see it. Visible progress is the engine.

---

## Rules to keep it clean

- A referral counts when the friend **attends**, not just when they sign up. This stops people from padding the list.
- Self-referrals and duplicates don't count.
- Be generous with recognition, careful with spend. The point is a bigger, better team, not a prize budget.
