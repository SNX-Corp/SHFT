06 / Gracious Professionalism

Gracious Professionalism.

It is the ethos of FIRST, and the way SHFT works: serious engineering, done with respect for the people around you.

We are building a team any student can join, no experience needed. Newcomers learn beside mentors who have built robots before. On event day we compete hard, then walk to the next pit and help a rival get running again.

Back SHFT and your name stands with a team learning to compete the right way: hard on the field, decent off it. That is the standard we are building toward.

Gracious Professionalism® was coined by Dr. Woodie Flowers (1943–2019), distinguished advisor to FIRST.
